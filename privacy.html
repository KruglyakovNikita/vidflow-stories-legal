Privacy Policy

VidFlow Stories is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The app asks for permission to upload videos and manage them on the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It uses an OAuth token for this account only.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data from Google accounts is collected from other people, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
